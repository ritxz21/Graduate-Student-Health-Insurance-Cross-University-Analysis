# Graduate Student Health Insurance — Cross-University Analysis

A cross-sectional analysis of graduate student health insurance plans at 50 U.S. universities, examining how plan features (premium, deductible, mental health coverage, dependent eligibility, etc.) vary by institutional characteristics — university type, graduate student union presence and strength, and degree-type plan differentiation (master's vs. PhD vs. professional).

## Project status

**Phase:** Foundation & Setup (Phase 1 of 4)
**Current week:** Week 3
**Timeline:** April 13 – June 30, 2025

## Research question

How do graduate student health insurance plans vary across U.S. universities, and to what extent is that variation explained by institutional characteristics?

## Sample

- 50 universities, balanced 25 public / 25 private
- Distributed across the four U.S. Census regions
- Drawn from six categories: Ivy League, R1 publics, mid-size privates, regional publics, liberal arts colleges, and professional-degree-heavy schools
